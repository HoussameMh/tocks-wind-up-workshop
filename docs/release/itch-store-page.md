## Description

> **Tock is a tiny clockwork toy with a very big problem.**
>
> Every night at dusk the workshop toys wake up. Tonight, Baron Rust, a vain old bench vise
> with a very small hat, has snatched the Golden Key that winds everyone up. Without it, the
> whole workshop winds down for good. Only Tock is small enough, and stubborn enough, to get
> it back.
>
> **Run, jump and stomp across five messy levels**
> - **Workbench Run**: benches, a moving platform and your first spring.
> - **Junk Drawer Dash**: pencil bridges, battery stepping stones and rubber-band launchers.
> - **Paint Shelf Panic**: wet paint that won't let you stop, and rubber ducks that snipe you while you slide.
> - **Toolbox Tumble**: giant wrenches that shuttle, lift and sweep, and chattering teeth that laugh at you.
> - **Baron Rust's Vise**: a three-phase boss fight of slams, thrown wrenches and flying dentures.
>
> **Power up**: the Coil Spring makes Tock bigger and bouncier, and the Bolt Blaster lets him
> fire back.
>
> **Find everything**: each of the first four levels hides three golden gears, under the
> course, on high ledges and behind the key. Beat your best times, unlock levels to replay
> from the level select, and chase a best run score.
>
> **Features**
> - Intro and outro films that tell Tock's story
> - Keyboard, mouse and gamepad support
> - Graphics quality setting: High for smooth edges and glow, Low for older laptops
> - Progress saves automatically
> - Reduced motion setting
>
> Made with the Godot Engine.
